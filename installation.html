Methods

How each floor goes down

Every product has its own fixing method and subfloor rules. These are the ones we work to, with typical laying times for a 60 m² home.

Floor How it's laid Subfloors Time for 60 m²
Hybrid Floating, click-lock, on attached or separate underlay Slab, timber, sound tiles 1–2 days
Laminate Floating on underlay with moisture barrier Slab, timber, sound tiles 1–2 days
Vinyl plank Glued down (2–3 mm) or click-lock (4–5 mm) Smooth slab, skimmed ply or particleboard 1–2 days
Engineered timber Floating, or direct-stick with flooring adhesive Slab, timber, some tiles 2–3 days
Solid timber Secret-nailed to joists or battens, or glued to slab, then sanded and coated Joists, battens, slab, ply 4–7 days + cure
Herringbone & chevron Direct-stick, set out from centre lines, with or without a border Slab, ply 3–5 days

Times cover laying only and assume the subfloor is ready. Levelling compound needs drying time before we lay over it.

Subfloor preparation

The part you never see, done properly

A floor is only as good as what it sits on. We assess the subfloor at the measure and price the prep it needs, so the quote you sign is the price you pay.

Newer homes, townhouses and apartments

Concrete slabs

  • Moisture tested, with a barrier fitted where the product needs one
  • High spots ground and old adhesive removed
  • Hollows filled with self-levelling compound
  • Cracks assessed and repaired before laying

Period homes, older houses, upper floors

Timber subfloors

  • Loose or squeaky boards re-fixed
  • Swollen or damaged particleboard replaced
  • Ply overlay where boards are too uneven to lay over
  • Ventilation checked under suspended floors

Kitchens, living areas, renovations

Over existing tiles

  • Tap-tested for drummy or loose tiles
  • Grout lines skimmed for glue-down vinyl
  • Door, stair and appliance heights checked
  • Removed and the slab prepared if not sound

Stairs

Stairs that match the floor

Carpeted stairs are the most common thing left behind in a reno. We clad treads and risers to match your new floor, so the look runs unbroken from one level to the next.

  • Hybrid & laminate stairsmatching nosing Treads and risers cut from your flooring, finished with a colour-matched stair nosing on every step.
  • Vinyl plank stairswrap or nosing Glue-down vinyl wraps neatly over the tread edge or finishes with an aluminium nosing for grip.
  • Timber stairssolid treads Bullnosed solid timber treads in the same species and finish as your floor.

Apartments, builders & landlords

Jobs with more rules than most

Apartments & strata

Many owners corporations set an acoustic rating for hard floors. We supply an underlay system tested to that rating, provide the test report for the building manager, protect lifts and common areas, and keep to the building's work hours.

Builders & developers

New homes, townhouse developments and multi-unit sites. We schedule around your trades, quote from plans, and supply to site on trade account terms.

Landlords & agents

Hard-wearing hybrid and vinyl that handle tenants and pets, laid fast between leases. Send the property details and we'll quote with access via the agent.
